Key Industry Trends
• Biologics & ADCs: Growth is heavily driven by complex biologics, antibody-drug conjugates (ADCs), and messenger RNA (mRNA) pipelines.
• Cell & Gene Therapy: Specialized providers like Lonza and Catalent continue to expand dedicated viral vector and cell therapy facilities.
• Geographic Expansion: Major players are investing heavily in localized manufacturing capacity across the U.S., Europe, and Asia to mitigate supply chain risks.
--
• Lonza: Best for end-to-end biopharma, small molecules, cell and gene therapies, and antibody-drug conjugates (ADCs).
• Samsung Biologics: Best for large-scale mammalian cell culture and massive commercial manufacturing output.
• Thermo Fisher Scientific (Patheon): Best for a broad global network, clinical trial supplies, and oral solid dosage forms.
• WuXi Biologics / WuXi AppTec: Best for fast-paced development, rapid scale-up, and integrated discovery-to-market pipelines.
• Catalent: Best for specialized drug delivery technologies (like Zydis fast-dissolve) and viral vectors.
The top 10 contract development and manufacturing organizations (CDMOs) worldwide:
• Lonza Group AG (Switzerland) – Globally recognized as the leading CDMO, specializing in mammalian biologics, cell and gene therapies, small molecules, and bioconjugates.
• Thermo Fisher Scientific (United States) – Operates its extensive CDMO services through its renowned Patheon brand, offering comprehensive small molecule and sterile fill-finish capabilities.
• Catalent (United States) – Now operating under Novo Holdings, it is a major provider of drug delivery solutions, advanced biologics, and oral solid dosages.
• Samsung Biologics (South Korea) – A global leader in massive industrial-scale mammalian biologics manufacturing, antibodies, and mRNA solutions.
• WuXi AppTec / WuXi STA (China) – Highly integrated CRDMO providing small molecule active pharmaceutical ingredient (API) development and advanced peptide lines.
• WuXi Biologics (China) – Focuses entirely on biologics, delivering end-to-end development for monoclonal antibodies (mAbs), bispecifics, and antibody-drug conjugates (ADCs).
• Siegfried Holding AG (Switzerland) – A critical network specialized in complex chemical custom synthesis, small-molecule APIs, and finished-dosage forms.
• Fujifilm Diosynth Biotechnologies (Japan/United States) – A global bio-CDMO partnering extensively with large pharma to scale up cell cultures, vaccines, and advanced biopharmaceuticals.
• Boehringer Ingelheim Biopharmaceuticals (Germany) – Recognized for its specialized BioXcellence platform, offering premier commercial manufacturing of mammalian and microbial cultures.
• Recipharm AB (Sweden) – A major European backbone for commercial drug production, leading in sterile fill-finish, advanced therapy medicinal products (ATMPs), and oral solids.
