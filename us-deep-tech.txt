Twist Bioscience ($TWST):
-------------------------
It is not simply a DNA synthesis company. It is an option-generation platform. Its silicon-based manufacturing system compounds scientific capability, enabling expansion into antibody discovery, synthetic biology, AI-enabled drug discovery and DNA data storage. The key question is not whether quarterly earnings beat expectations, but whether management is converting today's survival capital into tomorrow's option inventory faster than it is consuming it. As long as new opportunities accumulate faster than old ones expire, long-term expected value compounds through convexity rather than near-term profitability.
Caution: Biotech is a long-cycle business where scientific, regulatory and commercial workflows can span many years. Volatility is high, outcomes are uncertain, and randomness plays a much larger role than in most industries. Patience and appropriate position sizing are essential. No recommendation.
