Look for good management teams with ethics, hunger, aspiration and a willingness to work hard. These are the businesses that have the potential to create consistent wealth for shareholders.

Laurus Labs, Sansera Engineering, Sona BLW, Innomet Advanced and OBSC Perfection are a few examples.

Listen to their concalls, follow their media interactions and observe their behavior. Look for signs of inconsistency, lack of discipline, excessive storytelling or capital misallocation. Equally importantly, look for evidence that management walks the talk.

When you don't find these red flags and continue to see evidence of execution, you know you have a business worth shortlisting.

Once we shortlist a company, at what valuation to buy and at what valuation to sell is a personal choice, driven by conviction and data.

But perhaps we should change the sequence. Start with management, hunger and aspiration. Don't start with valuations and PAT.
