In addition, Sedemac is a global leader in **Genset Controllers (GCUs)** for North American/domestic standby power generators and is scaling sensorless **EV Traction Motor Controllers** and e-bike hub controllers.

Recently signed EV component partnerships to add DC-DC converters, on-board chargers, and rare-earth-free ferrite magnet motors.

  * **The ICE Ignition Trap:** Historically derived **>80% of revenue from 2W ICE Electronic Ignition systems** (Flywheel Magnetos, CDI units, Ignition Coils, and Regulators/Rectifiers for TVS, Hero, and Bajaj). When India transitioned from carburetors to BS-VI Electronic Fuel Injection (EFI) and then to EVs, simple CDIs became obsolete. ### MODULE 2: The Raw Material Basket & Margin Cyclicality Forensics

#### 1. Anatomy of the Tyre Raw Material Cost Basket (~62%–66% of Revenue)
Every 100 bps move in the composite raw material basket moves tyre EBITDA margins by **~60–65 bps** if unrecovered.
* **Natural Rubber (NR — ~32%–38% of RM Basket):** Higher weightage in Truck & Bus Bias/Radial (JK Tyre, Apollo) and 2W tyres (TVS Srichakra). Domestic RSS-4 Natural Rubber prices surged to **₹210–₹245/kg in FY25–FY26** due to domestic Kerala supply deficits and global EUDR (EU Deforestation Regulation) stocking, compounded by India's **25% (or ₹30/kg) customs duty on imported rubber** and inverted duty structures.
* **Crude Oil Derivatives (~50%–55% of RM Basket):**
  * **Synthetic Rubber (SBR / PBR — ~20–22%):** Higher weightage in Passenger Car Radials (PCR).
  * **Carbon Black (~15–18%):** Derived from Carbon Black Feedstock (CBFS). **BKT is the only tyre OEM with 100% backward integration** via its 3,60,000 MTPA captive carbon black plant at Bhuj (saving ~250–300 bps on gross margin and generating 9% of revenue from 3rd-party sales).
  * **Nylon Tyre Cord Fabric (NTCF) & Steel Tire Cord (~12–15%)** + **Rubber Chemicals (~5%)**.

#### 2. Pricing Power & Replacement Pass-Through Lag Audit
* **The Math of Pass-Through:** Because OEM contracts are indexed only to formulaic raw material movements (with a **1-quarter lag** and zero mark-up for conversion/energy inflation), tyre companies must implement a **~6%–7% price hike in the Replacement channel to offset a 10% spike in the overall RM basket**.
* **Price Leader vs. Price Taker Hierarchy:**
  * **MRF is the undisputed Price Setter in India.** Historically, Apollo, CEAT, and JK Tyre cannot sustain a >2% price hike in the TBR or 2W replacement bazaar unless MRF raises dealer invoice prices first (or unless MRF withholds hikes to squeeze leveraged peers—a tactic MRF used aggressively in FY22–23 and H1 FY26).
  * **BKT** exercises independent global pricing power—announcing **6%–8% price hikes in Q1 FY27** across global OHT channels because its tyres are still priced **15%–20% below Michelin, Bridgestone, and Trelleborg**.

#### 3. Input Supplier Pass-Through Forensics (Rajratan Global Wire vs. Triton Valves)
* **Rajratan Global Wire (Steel Wire Rod & Export Working Capital):**
  * High-carbon wire rod (sourced from JSW Steel, Tata Steel, and Chinese/ASEAN mills) makes up **~68%–72% of sales**. While Rajratan passes through steel costs quarterly, cheap Chinese bead wire dumping in Southeast Asia compressed its **Thailand plant EBITDA margin to 10%–11%**, pulling consolidated Q2 FY27 EBITDA margin down to **12.35%** (despite record quarterly volume of **38,605 MT, +17% YoY**). Worse, pushing exports to the US and Europe stretched receivable cycles to **70–80 days in Europe and 90 days in the US**, forcing higher working-capital borrowings.
* **Triton Valves (The Brass/Copper Working Capital Vice):**
  * **Copper and Zinc (Brass scrap/rods) constitute ~72%–75% of Triton's revenue.** Because Triton buys brass on near-cash/short credit terms from metal suppliers and sells valves on **60–90 day credit to giant tyre OEMs (MRF, Apollo, CEAT)** with a 1-quarter LME pass-through lag, any sharp spike in LME Copper/Zinc crushes its cash flows and keeps EBITDA margins thin at **~6.5%–7.4%** (FY26 EBITDA of ₹36.73 Cr on ₹561.48 Cr revenue = **6.54% margin**; Q3 FY26 margin improved to **7.40%**).

### MODULE 3: The EV Tailwind & Premiumization (Kit-Value & Realization Expansion)

#### 1. SUV Rim-Size Migration (12–14 Inch vs. 17–20 Inch Radials)
* As Indian PV sales shifted to 55% SUVs, tyre sizes migrated from 12–14 inch commuter radials (**ASP: ~₹3,200–₹4,200/tyre; EBITDA margin: ~8%–10%**) to **16–20 inch high-aspect-ratio SUV tyres** (**ASP: ~₹7,500–₹16,000/tyre; EBITDA margin: ~16%–22%**).
* **Apollo Tyres** (via *Apterra* in India and *Vredestein Ultrac/Quatrac* in Europe, where >65% of volume is 17-inch+ Ultra-High-Performance tyres) and **JK Tyre** (*Levitas Ultra* supercar/luxury tyres lifting PCR ASP by 8%–12%) are prime beneficiaries.

#### 2. EV-Specific Tyres (The 20% Faster Replacement Cycle)
* **Why EVs Are a Goldmine for Tyre Makers:** Battery Electric Vehicles weigh **20%–30% more** than equivalent ICE cars and deliver **100% instant starting torque**, causing tyres to **wear out 20%–25% faster** (shortening the replacement cycle from ~45,000 km to ~34,000–36,000 km!). Furthermore, EV tyres require **polyurethane acoustic foam linings** (to deaden cabin road noise in the absence of engine noise) and silica-rich low-rolling-resistance compounds, commanding a **15%–25% ASP premium**.
* **Market Share Leaders:** **CEAT** (*EnergyDrive* & *SecuraDrive EV* — >40% OEM share across Tata EVs, Mahindra EVs, MG ZS/Windsor, and Ola/Ather/TVS E-2Ws) and **MRF** (*Wanderer/Perfinza EV* & *Zapper EV*) lead domestic EV fitments.

#### 3. Triton Valves’ EV & Aluminum Pivot
* To escape low-margin brass tube valves, **Triton Valves** merged its wholly-owned subsidiary **Triton Valves Climatech Pvt Ltd** into the parent company (FY25–FY26) and scaled **anodized Aluminum TPMS (Tyre Pressure Monitoring System) valves** (which cost 3x–4x standard snap-in rubber/brass valves), **automotive HVAC service/expansion valves**, and **EV battery pack pressure-relief/cooling valves**. This drove Triton's Q3 FY26 net profit up **+289% YoY to ₹4.16 Cr** and FY26 PAT up **+46% YoY to ₹8.12 Cr** on ₹561.48 Cr revenue.

### 🔍 Case Study 2: The OEM Insourcing Trap — Sterling Tools vs. BorgWarner & Tesla/Rivian Zonal Architecture
**Indian Protagonists:** *Sterling Tools (Sterling Gtake E-Mobility - SGEM) vs. Sedemac Mechatronics & Pricol*  
**US Counterparts:** *BorgWarner Inc. (NYSE: BWA) & Visteon Corp (NASDAQ: VC)*

#### 1. Why Sterling Tools De-Rated to ₹790 Cr Market Cap (1.5x P/B) While Sedemac & Pricol Compounded
In 2022–2023, retail investors bid up **Sterling Tools** on the narrative that its subsidiary **SGEM** was India’s largest EV Motor Control Unit (MCU) supplier. By 2025–2026, SGEM ran headfirst into the exact same wall that hit **BorgWarner (US, trading at ~7x–8x P/E)**:
* **The US Lesson (Why EV OEMs Insource Inverters/MCUs):** In an internal combustion vehicle, OEMs happily outsourced turbochargers and alternators to BorgWarner. In an EV, the **Traction Inverter / Motor Controller (MCU)** directly governs vehicle range, acceleration curve, and Silicon-Carbide (SiC) thermal efficiency. Consequently, **Tesla, Rivian (in its Gen-2 R1 architecture, which eliminated 10 ECUs and 1.6 miles of wiring), Hyundai, and BYD all designed their inverters and BMS in-house**!
* **The Indian Replication:** In India, **Ola Electric, Ather Energy, TVS Motor, and Bajaj Auto** all insourced MCU hardware/firmware design for their Gen-2/Gen-3 E-2Ws to capture PLI incentives and control vehicle software—leaving **Sterling Tools (which relied on licensed Chinese IP from Jiangsu Gtake)** scrambling for smaller LCV/E-3W accounts.
* **Why Sedemac Mechatronics Is Immune:** Unlike SGEM’s licensed Chinese assembly model, **Sedemac** invented and patented **Sensorless Commutation (SLC)** at IIT Bombay—eliminating physical Hall-effect sensors inside 2W Integrated Starter Generators (ISGs) and traction motors. Because TVS, Bajaj, and US genset giant **Generac** cannot replicate Sedemac’s patented back-EMF estimation firmware without patent infringement, Sedemac enjoys true **Owned Margins (18%–20.5% EBITDA margin, PAT up 8.3x in 3 years)**.
