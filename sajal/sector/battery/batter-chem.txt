What are battery chemicals?

A lithium-ion battery is made up of several important components.

Four critical components are:

• Cathode: Determines energy capacity and performance.
• Anode: Stores and releases lithium ions.
• Electrolyte: Allows lithium ions to move between electrodes.
• Separator: Keeps the electrodes apart and prevents short circuits.

Speciality chemical companies manufacture several of these materials

Why is this opportunity growing?

The demand for batteries is increasing because of:

Electric vehicles
Renewable energy storage
Grid-scale battery storage
Consumer electronics

India is also trying to reduce its dependence on imported battery materials and build a domestic supply chain.

This creates an opportunity for Indian chemical manufacturers.


Neogen Chemicals – Building a battery chemicals business

Neogen is expanding into lithium-ion battery materials through its subsidiary, Neogen Ionics.

Its focus areas include:

• Lithium electrolyte salts.
• Battery electrolytes.
• Electrolyte additives.

These materials are critical for the performance and functioning of lithium-ion batteries.
Q2 FY27:

• Battery chemicals revenue: ₹37 crore versus ₹5.4 crore a year ago.
• Consolidated revenue: ₹296 crore, up 42% YoY.
• EBITDA: ₹45 crore, up 51% YoY.
• Net profit: ₹8 crore.

Management expects further scaling as domestic battery manufacturing capacity comes online and global customers diversify sourcing.

This is a business worth tracking as capacity utilisation improves.

Himadri Speciality Chemical – Moving beyond traditional chemicals

Himadri is building a presence across multiple battery materials.

Its focus areas include:

• Lithium Iron Phosphate (LFP) cathode materials.
• Synthetic graphite anode materials.
• Carbon nanotubes.

The company has already started operations at its 200 MTPA anode material facility.

It is targeting the growing global market for battery-grade materials.

Himadri – Big capacity expansion plans

Some important developments:

• 200 MTPA anode material facility has commenced operations.
• 2,000 MTPA LFP demonstration capacity is targeted for Q3 FY27.
• 40,000 MTPA LFP commercial capacity is planned.
• 200 MTPA carbon nanotube facility is targeted for Q4 FY27.

Q2 FY27 financial performance:

• Revenue: ₹1,691 crore, up 58% YoY.
• EBITDA: ₹315 crore.
• Net profit: ₹231 crore, up 31% YoY.

The battery business is still developing, but the expansion plans are ambitious.

Gujarat Fluorochemicals – An integrated battery materials opportunity

Gujarat Fluorochemicals is entering the battery supply chain through GFCL EV.

Its product portfolio includes:

• LiPF6 – Electrolyte salt.
• PVDF and PTFE – Battery binders.
• LFP – Cathode materials.
• Electrolyte formulations and additives.

The interesting part is that the company is targeting multiple stages of the battery manufacturing value chain

Gujarat Fluorochemicals – Commercial progress

The company has reported encouraging developments:

• LiPF6 sales have started, with supplies to five major global electrolyte players.
• LFP samples are progressing through customer qualification.
• PVDF and PTFE grades are undergoing customer qualification.
• Commercial supplies under long-term agreements for certain binder products are expected from Q4 FY27.

Q2 FY27 battery materials revenue stood at ₹37 crore.

The EV products segment also reported an EBITDA loss of ₹32 crore, showing that the business is still in its investment and scaling-up phase.

What should investors track?

These companies operate in different parts of the battery supply chain.

But the real opportunity will depend on execution.

Watch these five things:

Commercial production and capacity utilisation.
Customer approvals and long-term contracts.
Revenue contribution from battery materials.
EBITDA margins and return on capital.

Final takeaway

Neogen Chemicals, Himadri Speciality Chemical and Gujarat Fluorochemicals are three Indian companies building exposure to the battery materials opportunity.

But remember:

A large capex announcement does not automatically translate into profits.

The companies that successfully scale production, qualify with customers and generate sustainable returns will be the ones to watch.
