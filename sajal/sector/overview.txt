Michaele porter 5 forces:
-------------------------
Michael Porter’s 5 forces that shape the Industry’s Structure:
1.Competitive Intensity (Rivalry)
2.Bargaining power of Firms relative to their Suppliers and Buyers
3.Threat of new Entrants
4.Threat of Substitutes
5.Value of product / services to Buyers / Customers
