🔍 2. High-Value Institutional Parameters We Added (And Why They Matter)
For Indian Industrials and EPC companies, standard P&L analysis often misses balance sheet traps. Here are the high-impact forensic metrics now built into your prompt and why you should track them:

 Parameter Category	Specific Metric Added	Why Institutional Investors Track It in Indian Industrials
Order Book Quality	Fixed-Price vs. PVC Split (%)	When steel, copper, or aluminium spikes, companies with >50% fixed-price orders suffer severe gross margin compression, while PVC (Price Variation Clause) players pass costs through.
Order Book Quality	Order Inflow Growth vs. Backlog Growth	High backlog growth can sometimes mask execution delays (denominator effect). Tracking fresh Order Inflows alongside Order Book/Bill Ratio prevents falling into stalled-project traps.
Revenue Quality	Aftermarket / Spares & Service Mix (%)	In Capital Goods (Pumps, Compressors, Bearings, Turbines), initial equipment sales carry 12–15% margins, whereas aftermarket spares/O&M carry 30–45% annuity margins and smooth out cyclicality.
Working Capital Forensics	Unbilled Revenue (Contract Assets) & Retention Money	Many EPC and Defence companies report low "Trade Receivable Days" on paper by shifting delayed milestone payments into "Other Financial Assets / Unbilled Revenue." Combining Receivable Days + Contract Assets gives the true cash collection cycle.
Working Capital Forensics	Customer Advances (% of Order Book)	Companies with strong bargaining power (e.g., niche turbine, transformer, or process equipment makers) receive 15–30% interest-free customer advances, funding their working capital and boosting ROCE to 35%+.
Operating Leverage	Fixed Asset Turnover (Gross Block Turns) & Capacity Utilization (%)	Tells you how much incremental revenue a company can generate without raising fresh debt or equity, and whether margins are at peak-cycle utilization (85%+) or mid-cycle (60%).
Off-Balance Sheet Risk	Non-Fund Based Limits (Bank Guarantees / LCs as % of Net Worth)	Industrial & EPC firms rely heavily on Bank Guarantees (BGs) for bidding and performance warranties. If BG utilization nears 100%, order booking stalls even if demand is booming.

 ⚠️ 3. Watch Out For: Common Pitfalls When Running This Prompt
Never ask an AI to generate all 14–15 parts in a single response: Even with the largest context windows, asking for 15 sectors × 10 companies × 20 columns = 3,000+ data cells in one turn will cause the model to summarize superficially. Always run it Part-by-Part (e.g., "Execute Part 1: Aerospace & Defence using the Master Prompt").

Cyclical Peak-Multiple Trap (Mental Model Check): In deep cyclicals (Part 2 T&D, Part 8 Steel/Pipes, Part 9 CVs), a low P/E or EV/EBITDA often signals peak cycle margins, whereas a high P/E during a capex downturn may signal depressed trough earnings. The prompt's 10-year vs. current margin comparison protects you against paying peak multiples on peak margins.
