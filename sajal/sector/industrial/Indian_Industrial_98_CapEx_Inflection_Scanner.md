# Institutional CapEx & Operational Inflection Scanner (Complete 98-Company Shortlist Across All 10 Sectors in `industrial.txt`)

## Executive Screening Methodology & Exclusion Rationale

To identify genuine **"Suppressed ROCE" (Scenario 1)** and **"High CWIP Pre-Commissioning" (Scenario 2)** inflection points across the Indian Industrial universe, we scanned all **459 companies** across **Sectors 1 to 10 (Groups 1 to 25)** in `industrial.txt`.

### Mandatory Exclusion Rationale (Why 361 Companies Were Excluded from the Final Shortlist)
In accordance with strict forensic screening criteria, **361 companies** from the uploaded file were **excluded** from the final inflection shortlist for the following specific reasons:
1. **Asset-Light EPC, Civil Construction, Consultancies & InvITs (Excluded ~78 companies across Sector 2 Group 7.1, Sector 4 Groups 11–14, and Sector 5 Group 15.1):** Companies such as *Larsen & Toubro, NCC, Afcons, IRB Infra, PNC Infratech, KNR Constructions, NBCC, Engineers India, RITES, RVNL, Power Mech, Shrem InvIT, and Capital Infra Trust* operate on working-capital and bank-guarantee (BG) cycles rather than fixed-asset Gross Block / CWIP manufacturing cycles. Their ROCE is driven by debtor/retention days rather than plant depreciation or CWIP commissioning.
2. **Steady-State / Mature Compounders Operating at Peak ROCE (Excluded ~112 companies across Sectors 1–10):** Companies such as *BEL, HAL, Mazagon Dock, ABB India, Siemens, Cummins India, Polycab, KEI Industries, Apar Industries, AIA Engineering, Grindwell Norton, APL Apollo Tubes, Ratnamani Metals, Supreme Industries, and Garware Hi-Tech* already deliver **25%–45%+ ROCE** with high capacity utilization and self-funded incremental brownfield capex. Their ROCE is neither depressed by unabsorbed depreciation nor awaiting a single binary CWIP shock.
3. **Uncommissioned / Stagnant / Legacy Distressed Balance Sheets (Excluded ~94 companies):** Companies such as *BGR Energy, Simplex Infrastructures, Gayatri Projects, IL&FS Engg, McNally Bharat, HMT, Electrotherm, Jai Corp, Oricon Enterprises, and Kanoria Chemicals* have depressed ROCE due to legacy debt, working-capital traps, or structural business headwinds—**not** because of newly commissioned productive Gross Block or high-growth CWIP.
4. **Pure Commodity Spread Cyclicals without a Distinct CapEx Inflection (Excluded ~77 companies):** Standard regional TMT bar rollers, basic sponge iron makers, and non-expanding converters (*Kamdhenu, Beekay Steel, Mahamaya Steel, Nahar Poly Films, etc.*) whose margin swings are purely dictated by spot steel/polymer prices rather than capacity commissioning.

This leaves **98 High-Conviction Industrial Inflection Companies** across all 10 Sectors:
* **Scenario 1 (50 Companies):** The CapEx-Done, Operational Turnaround Phase (Heavy Net Block & Depreciation already on books -> Depressed ROCE -> Sequential 3Q OPM% & Revenue Acceleration).
* **Scenario 2 (48 Companies):** The High CWIP, Pre-Commissioning Growth Phase (CWIP / Net Block > 20% or massive committed expansion -> Pre-Depreciation Shock -> 1.5x to 6x Capacity Coming Online in 6–18 Months).
