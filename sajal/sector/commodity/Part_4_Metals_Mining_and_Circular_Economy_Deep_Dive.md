### MODULE 1: Executive Summary, Value-Chain Positioning & Variant Perception

- **Consensus vs. Reality:** What is the current market consensus on [Company Name], where is the street mispricing the commodity cycle or structural conversion spreads, and what is our differentiated variant perception?

- **Value-Chain Classification (Crucial First Step):** Clearly classify what percentage of revenue and EBITDA comes from:

  1. *Upstream Resource / Integrated Primary Producer* (Captive ore/bauxite/coal + smelting—exposed to absolute LME/steel prices)

  2. *Downstream Converter / Processor* (Tubes, pipes, foils, extrusions, specialty alloys—exposed to conversion spreads and inventory gains/losses)

  3. *Secondary Recycler / Urban Miner* (Lead, aluminum, copper, plastic, lithium-ion scrap—exposed to scrap-to-LME spreads, tolling margins, and EPR regulations)

- **Thesis Categorization:** Is this a "1st-Quartile Cost-Curve Compounder," a "Structural Value-Added Spread Play," a "Circular Economy Regulatory Beneficiary," or a "Peak-Cycle Balance Sheet Value Trap"?

### MODULE 2: Resource Security, Scrap Procurement Moats & Asset Architecture

- **For Primary Miners & Integrated Producers:**

  - **Captive Ore & Reserve Longevity:** Detail captive mine reserves (Iron ore, Bauxite, Zinc-Lead, Copper, Thermal/Coking Coal), stripping ratios, ore grades/head grades (and grade depletion trends), and remaining mine life at post-expansion run-rates.

  - **Auctioned vs. Legacy Mines:** Break down the split between legacy low-royalty mines vs. newly auctioned mines (quantify the Revenue Share / Auction Premium % impact per tonne and license expiry dates under the MMDR Act).

- **For Secondary Recyclers:**

  - **Scrap Sourcing Moat:** Break down scrap procurement: Domestic organized (OEM/institutional batteries/scrap) vs. Domestic unorganized vs. Overseas captive collection yards (e.g., Africa, Middle East, Europe, US).

  - **Regulatory & EPR Tailwinds:** Quantify the impact of Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR), Battery Waste Management Rules (BWMR), Reverse Charge Mechanism (RCM) on GST, and customs duty differentials. Is the formalization shift genuinely driving volume from unorganized smelters to [Company Name]?

- **Plant Location & Logistics Architecture:** Analyze proximity to ports (for coking coal/scrap imports and finished exports), pit-heads, and consumption clusters, alongside captive railway sidings and slurry pipelines.

### MODULE 3: Per-Tonne Unit Economics, Spreads & Cost-Curve Forensics (5-Year Trend)

Build a 5-year per-tonne table ($/tonne or Rs/tonne) and dissect the margin drivers:

1. **Realization & Spread Mechanics:**

   - Track Net Sales Realization (NSR/tonne), Regional/Physical Premiums over LME/SHFE, Value-Added Product (VAP) share %, and Gross Conversion Spread/tonne (Realization minus Raw Material cost/tonne).

   - For multi-metal producers, quantify **By-Product Credits** (e.g., Silver in Zinc-Lead, Gold/Sulfuric Acid in Copper, Vanadium/Slag in Steel) and how much they lower the net cost of production.

2. **Conversion & Energy Cost Forensics:**

   - Analyze Power & Fuel Cost/tonne (critical for Aluminum, Ferro-alloys, and Zinc smelting): Captive thermal coal linkage vs. e-auction coal vs. imported coal vs. Renewable/Round-the-Clock (RTC) green power share.

   - Analyze reductant/flux costs (Coking coal blend, PCI injection rates, Coke rate in kg/tonne of hot metal, Carbon anode costs).

3. **Hedging & Inventory Distortion Check:**

   - **Inventory Gains/Losses:** Strip out FIFO accounting inventory gains/losses during sharp commodity spikes/crashes to reveal true **Underlying Operating EBITDA/tonne**.

   - **Hedging Policy (Vital for Recyclers & Converters):** Does the company maintain a strict "back-to-back" LME hedging book (fixing the spread between scrap purchase and metal sale), or does management take speculative directional bets on LME prices?

4. **Rented vs. Owned Margins:** Rank the company on the global/domestic cost curve (C1/C3 cash cost quartile). Isolate how much of the 5-year EBITDA/tonne expansion is **"Owned"** (captive mines, slurry pipelines, downstream VAP mix, backward integration, overseas scrap yards) vs. **"Rented"** (cyclical LME spikes, Chinese supply cuts, anti-dumping duties/MIP protections, or temporary Russian/discounted coal arbitrage).

### MODULE 4: Capital Allocation, Cycle Timing & Forensic Accounting

- **Capex Efficiency & Counter-Cyclical Discipline:** Detail all ongoing/announced expansions (MTPA/KTPA, blast furnace vs. EAF/DRI vs. smelter vs. recycling plants). Calculate Capital Cost per Tonne ($/tonne) vs. global replacement benchmarks. Did management initiate heavy capex at the top of the commodity cycle or invest counter-cyclically?

- **The Harvest Test & Through-Cycle ROIC:** Over a full 7-to-10-year commodity cycle, compare cumulative Incremental NOPAT and Free Cash Flow (FCF) against cumulative Capex + Acquisitions. Calculate Through-Cycle ROIC vs. WACC.

- **Working Capital & Cash Conversion Forensics:**

  - Track 5-year OCF-to-EBITDA (%) conversion.

  - Scrutinize the Cash Conversion Cycle: Raw material transit inventory (especially imported coking coal or sea-borne scrap), finished goods piling, and off-balance-sheet **Acceptances / Vendor Financing / Letter of Credit (LC) discounting** (which often masks true debt in steel and metal companies).

- **Parent-Subsidiary & Holding Company Forensics:**

  - Check for high-cost overseas acquisitions (e.g., European/UK/US downstream assets) dragging down domestic cash cows.

  - Check for upstreaming of cash via inter-corporate loans, brand fees, or aggressive dividends to service Promoter Holding Company debt.

  - Quantify contingent liabilities (mining tax/royalty disputes, environmental/NGT closures, tailing dam liabilities).

### MODULE 5: Management Quality, Execution & Governance Audit

- **"Say vs. Do" Audit:** Compare management’s past 5 years of guidance on project commissioning timelines, captive mine ramp-up, VAP share targets, and debt-reduction promises against actual delivery.

- **Leverage & Balance Sheet Survival Test:** What is the company’s True Net Debt (including acceptances, bill discounting, and customer advances) to Mid-Cycle EBITDA? If LME/steel spreads drop to 10-year trough levels for 6 consecutive quarters, does the company breach debt covenants?

- **Governance & RPT Forensics:** Audit Related Party Transactions (RPTs)—specifically checking whether mining MDO (Mine Developer & Operator) contracts, scrap trading entities, logistics fleets, or overseas trading subsidiaries in Dubai/Singapore/Mauritius are routed through promoter-affiliated entities.

- **ESG, Decarbonization & CBAM Readiness:** Evaluate exposure to the EU Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM), Scope 1 & 2 emissions per tonne of metal, scrap utilization rates, and workplace safety/fatality records.

### MODULE 6: Multi-Lens Valuation & Cycle Normalization

Standard P/E ratios are inverted in cyclical metals (stocks look cheapest on P/E at the cyclical peak when earnings are unsustainable, and most expensive at the trough). Perform a cycle-adjusted valuation:

1. **Normalized Mid-Cycle EV/EBITDA & EV/Tonne:**

   - Estimate **Mid-Cycle Sustainable EBITDA/tonne** (stripping out peak commodity windfalls and trough inventory write-downs) applied to FY27E/FY28E operational volumes.

   - Calculate Current and Forward **EV/Tonne** vs. Greenfield Replacement Cost and historical 10-year trading bands (Price-to-Book and EV/Invested Capital vs. ROE/ROIC).

2. **Sum-of-the-Parts (SOTP) Valuation:** Where applicable, value Upstream Mining/Smelting (at cyclical 4x–6x mid-cycle EV/EBITDA), Downstream Value-Added/Structural Tubes (at converter 10x–15x EV/EBITDA), and Recycling/Circular Economy divisions separately, subtracting True Net Debt (inclusive of acceptances).

3. **Implied Expectations (Reverse DCF):** At the current Enterprise Value (assuming 12–13% WACC), back-solve for the implied sustainable EBITDA/tonne and volume CAGR required over the next 10 years to justify today's valuation.

4. **3-Year Bull / Base / Bear IRR Matrix:** Build a scenario table flexing:

   - Volume ramp-up / Utilization (%)

   - Sustainable EBITDA/tonne (or Conversion Spread/tonne)

   - Exit Multiple (EV/EBITDA and P/B)

   Derive the implied Target Share Price, Market Cap, and 3-year IRR.

## MODULE 2: Resource Security, Scrap Procurement Moats & Asset Architecture

### 2.1 Primary Miners & Integrated Producers: Captive Ore & Auction Premium Forensics
Under India’s amended **MMDR Act**, the single biggest determinant of long-term metal ROCE is whether a company operates **pre-2015 legacy mining leases** (paying standard 15% IBM royalty) or **post-2015 auctioned blocks** (where aggressive bidding resulted in **95% to 135% revenue-share auction premiums**, stripping out all upstream mining rents):
* **The Zero-Premium Legacy Moat Winners:**
  * **Lloyds Metals (#13):** Holds the **Surjagarh Iron Ore lease in Gadchiroli (Maharashtra) valid until 2057 with ZERO additional auction premium**, expanded to **55 MTPA Environmental Clearance (EC)** capacity. Combined with its **85 km Hedri-to-Konsari slurry pipeline** (saving **₹500–550/tonne** in road freight), Lloyds sits at the absolute bottom of the global iron ore delivered cost curve.
  * **NALCO (#19) & Hindalco (#6):** NALCO’s Panchpatmali and newly allotted **Pottangi Bauxite Mines (3.5 MTPA)** plus Utkal D & E coal blocks give it 100% bauxite and ~75%+ coal security at base royalty. Hindalco’s captive bauxite mines + Chakla/Meenakshi coal mines + **India’s first 65 MW Round-the-Clock (RTC) captive renewable plant at Aditya Aluminium** (643 MW total RE) lock in 1st-quartile smelting power.
  * **Hindustan Zinc (#4):** Holds long-term underground leases at **Rampura Agucha, Sindesar Khurd (high silver grade), Zawar, Rajpura Dariba, and Kayad** (~25+ year reserve/resource life at 1.2 MTPA mined metal rate) plus the newly won **Gundlupet Rare Earth Elements (REE) & Yttrium block in Karnataka (Q1FY27)**.
  * **Tata Steel (#5) & SAIL (#17):** Tata Steel enjoys 100% captive iron ore in Odisha/Jharkhand; as its legacy Noamundi/Joda leases face 2030 renewal at auction/statutory premiums, its acquisition of **NINL (Kalinganagar)** secured long-tenure captive iron ore reserves.
  * **Sarda Energy (#44) & Sandur Manganese (#66):** Sarda secured captive coal (Gare Palma IV/7, Shahpur West) and won the **Avagudem Manganese Block (AP)** in H1FY27; Sandur holds low-cost legacy manganese and iron ore leases in Karnataka generating a **51.8% mining EBIT margin**.

### 2.2 Secondary Recyclers: Scrap Sourcing Moat & EPR Regulatory Tailwinds
* **Overseas Captive Scrap Yards vs. Port Imports (The Gravita Moat):**
  * **Gravita India (#57)** procures **~45–50%+ of its scrap via its own overseas collection and smelting network** across **Ghana, Senegal, Mozambique (expanded Oct 2026), Tanzania, Togo, Costa Rica, Dominican Republic, Romania, Sri Lanka, and newly incorporated Gravita Recyclers USA Inc. (Oct 2026)**. * **EPR, BWMR & Reverse Charge Mechanism (RCM) Tailwinds:** Strict enforcement of India's **Battery Waste Management Rules (BWMR)**, **Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) certificates**, and **GST Reverse Charge Mechanism (RCM) on metal scrap** has crippled unorganized backyard lead smelters, shifting OEM battery scrap from Exide/Amara Raja and telecom/UPS towers directly to formal recyclers.

### 7.2 Inversion / Pre-Mortem (Munger Framework: What Causes a 40% Drawdown Over 3 Years?)

1. **Cross-Border Capital Allocation Misstep (Hits Lloyds Metals & Hindalco):**
   * *The Failure Mechanism:* Domestic Indian mining cash cows have historically destroyed shareholder wealth when deploying surplus cash into complex overseas assets (e.g., Tata Steel Corus in 2007). For **Lloyds Metals (#13)**, its domestic Gadchiroli iron ore + slurry pipeline is a pristine 39% margin fortress, but acquiring **Chemaf’s DRC Congo Copper/Cobalt mines** has lifted consolidated net debt to **~₹19,000 Cr**. If DRC resource nationalism, cobalt oversupply, or tax disputes stall Chemaf cash flows, consolidated EV/EBITDA will de-rate sharply. For **Hindalco (#6)**, if Novelis’s $4.1B Bay Minette project faces a further $1B capex overrun while North American beverage can spreads compress, consolidated Net Debt (₹80,435 Cr) could breach its 2.0x EBITDA covenant ceiling.
2. **Reversal of the Silver & Alumina Spike + State Mineral Tax Contagion (Hits Hindustan Zinc, Vedanta, NALCO & NMDC):**
   * *The Failure Mechanism:* **85% of Vedanta/Hindustan Zinc's YoY Q2FY27 EBITDA surge came from spot LME Zinc ($3,825/t), Silver ($62.6/oz), and USD/INR (₹95.57)**, while **NALCO's** record profits were supercharged by **$375–400/t spot Alumina**. If global industrial demand slows—pulling Silver back to $35/oz, Zinc to $2,600/t, and Alumina to $300/t as Indonesian/Guinean alumina refineries come online—while mineral-rich states (Odisha, Chhattisgarh, Rajasthan, Karnataka) enact new state-level mineral land taxes, upstream EBITDA will drop 35–45% from H1FY27 peaks.
3. **Global Scrap Export Bans & Container Disruption (Hits Gravita, Jain Resource & Pondy Oxides):**
   * *The Failure Mechanism:* As seen in Q2FY27, West Asia geopolitical disruptions caused **Gravita India's lead volumes to drop 11% YoY**, while tightening global copper scrap discounts compressed **Jain Resource Recycling's EBITDA margin by 340 bps to 4.16%**. If European, Middle Eastern, and African governments impose protectionist **export bans or heavy export duties on battery, copper, and aluminium scrap** to retain critical minerals domestically for green-transition goals, Indian recyclers will face structural raw-material starvation and shrinking scrap-to-LME spreads.
