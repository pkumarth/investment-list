Nothing rerates like a Scarcity Premium!

Transformers went from being deep commodity businesses to commanding 100x PE multiples.

Memory companies witnessed a once-in-a-lifetime rerating despite being in what was traditionally considered a commodity business.

Solar EPC companies went from being relatively unknown businesses to commanding valuations of 100x PE.

We can all have our own views and opinions on valuations, but the market rewards scarcity. And when supply cannot catch up with demand, scarcity premium kicks in!

However, the flip side is equally important. The premium can vanish within a matter of weeks or months.

So, play the scarcity premium well, but know what drives it and track those drivers closely. Don’t be quick in exiting and don’t overstay your welcome!
