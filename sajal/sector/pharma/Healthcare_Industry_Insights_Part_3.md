While retail and momentum funds piled into Divi’s in 2025–2026—driving the stock up 66% in a year to ₹9,400–9,615 (~86x P/E)—Aditya Khemka explicitly explained in his **April 24, 2025** and **July 14, 2025 *Economic Times* interviews** why he refuses to buy Divi’s at these multiples:

  * While the street is paying 86x P/E for Divi’s based on its solid/liquid-phase peptide synthesis and amino-acid fragments for GLP-1 drugs (Semaglutide/Tirzepatide), Khemka warned that **every Chinese and Indian API/peptide player is building massive GLP-1 capacity simultaneously**, which risks creating **oversupply and margin collapse** once patents expire, rather than sustainable monopoly profits.

#### **2. Forensic Look at Divi’s Q1 FY27 "42.8% Margin" Print (Both Khemka & Sajal’s Lens)**

Stripping out that inventory change, **underlying adjusted gross margins were ~55–58% and adjusted EBITDA margins were ~28–30%**! That is why management guided full-year FY27 EBITDA margins to revert closer to FY26 levels rather than sustaining 43%.

#### **2. How That Exact "Double-Whammy" Hit in Q1 FY27 (July 2026)**

3. **FY27 "Year of Transition" & FY28 Recovery Path:** Under new MD & CEO **Siddharth Mittal** (after Jonathan Hunt stepped down), management cut FY27 guidance to a **single-digit INR revenue decline** with mid-20s full-year EBITDA margins—making **FY28** the true inflection year once the US **Bayview facility** (operationalized by end-FY27), **Stelis Unit III**, and the **Mangalore small-molecule API plant** fill their idle capacity with diversified CDMO clients.
