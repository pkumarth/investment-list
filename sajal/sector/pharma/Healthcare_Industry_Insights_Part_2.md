### **Part 2: Deep Dive into Alkem Laboratories via Khemka’s Lens**
*(Market Cap: ~₹63,600 Cr | FY26 Revenue: ₹14,712 Cr | FY26 EBITDA: ₹3,005 Cr [20.4% Margin] | Pre-Tax ROCE: **29.6%** | Net Cash: **₹5,764 Cr**)*

#### **1. The Unassailable Domestic Acute + Trade Generics Fortress (67.8% of Revenue)**
Khemka has tracked Alkem for over a decade (regularly interrogating MD Sandeep Singh, CFO Rajesh Dubey, and Chronic head Yogesh Kaushal on concalls):
* **Megabrand Velocity:** In India, Alkem’s acute mother brands—**Clavam** (Amoxycillin-Clavulanate), **Pan-40 / Pan-D** (Pantoprazole), **Taxim / Taxim-O**, **A to Z**, **Gemcal**, and **Sumo**—have consumer-like recall among general practitioners and surgeons.
* **Scaling the Chronic Division (35% of Field Force):** On Alkem’s Q2 FY20 and Q2 FY21 calls, Khemka tracked how Alkem expanded its field force (now **14,500+ MRs**) by putting 35–40% of new MRs into Neuro/CNS, Cardiac, and Anti-Diabetes—covering over 100,000 specialist doctors and growing Chronic at 1.5x–2x the rate of Acute.
* **Carving Out Trade Generics (*Alkem Wellness*):** Alkem is India’s pioneer and largest player in Trade Generics. Effective October 1, 2025, Alkem carved out its Trade Generics business into a wholly owned subsidiary (*Alkem Wellness Limited*) and enforced strict receivable and inventory discipline in Q1 FY27.

#### **2. Why Khemka Scrutinizes Alkem’s Overseas Capital Allocation (US Generics, *Enzene*, & *Occlutech*)**
While Alkem’s standalone domestic business generates a **27.61% operating margin** (as of Q1 FY27), its consolidated EBITDA margin sits lower at **20.4%–20.5%**. Why?
1. **The US Biologics CDMO Drag (*Enzene Biosciences*):** Alkem set up a continuous-manufacturing biologics CDMO plant in Hopewell, New Jersey (USA). In Q1 FY27, the US *Enzene* unit incurred **~₹60 crore per quarter (~₹240 Cr/year) in operating expenses**, and management pushed back its $25–30M revenue breakeven timeline from FY27 to **FY28**.
2. **MedTech Diversification (*Occlutech*):** Alkem ventured into structural heart medical devices via *Occlutech* (targeting ₹400 Cr revenue in FY27 at breakeven EBITDA) and orthopedic implants (*Exactech India*).
* **Khemka’s View:** Under Khemka’s framework, Alkem’s domestic core is a **30%+ ROCE cash machine** with **₹5,764 Cr in net cash**, but management’s habit of deploying domestic cash into overseas CDMO (*Enzene* US) and MedTech dilutions caps consolidated ROE/ROCE expansion compared to pure-play domestic compounders.

#### **3. Aditya Khemka’s Verbatim Concall Interrogations on Alkem Labs**

   > **Aditya Khemka:** *"In terms of gross margin of 60% in FY2020 compared to our FY2019 gross margin, our margins are already expanding by 4% to 5% year-over-year. And then why should that not translate to higher EBITDA expansion [beyond 100–125 bps] is what I am struggling with?"*

Firstly on the asset utilization side, so we talked US as a growth engine, where do we stand on an average on our asset utilization for US? ### **Part 3: Deep Dive into Indoco Remedies via Khemka & Sajal’s Lenses**
*(Market Cap: ~₹2,095 Cr | Former Top-5 InCred Holding [5.1%–8% Weight] $\rightarrow$ Exited in Late 2024)*

#### **1. Why Khemka Originally Owned Indoco as a Top-5 Pick (2018–2023)**
In his December 21, 2023 *Economic Times* interview, Khemka named **Indoco Remedies** among his **Top 5 holdings** (alongside RPG Life, Thyrocare, Krsnaa, and HCG). His thesis rested on three pillars:
* **The Crown-Jewel Domestic Dental/Stomatological Franchise:** Indoco derives ~50–55% of its revenue from India, where it dominates **Stomatologicals (medicated dental care)** through doctor-prescribed **Sensodent-K**, **Sensodent-KF**, **Rexidin**, and **Kidodent** (growing 16%–18% YoY with consumer-like repeat usage and high gross margins), alongside **Cital / Cital-UTI** (>₹100 Cr brand growing 19% YoY) and **Cyclopam**.
* **The Complex Ophthalmic & Sterile US Pipeline (Partnered with Teva):** Unlike plain-vanilla oral generics, Indoco built sterile ophthalmic and injectable lines at **Goa Plant II and Plant III** (*Brimonidine*, *Brinzolamide*, *Allopurinol*) partnered with global giants like Teva.
* **The SOTP Thesis:** Khemka’s thesis was that once Goa Plant II & III cleared their USFDA Warning Letter/OAI status, the ₹4+ Cr/quarter of unabsorbed sterile plant remediation/depreciation drag would flip into high-margin US ophthalmic profit, while the domestic dental franchise compounded steadily.

#### **2. Why Khemka Exited Indoco Remedies (Verbatim January 4, 2025 Explanation)**
Between 2018 and 2024, Indoco spent **₹861 crore on CapEx** (~60% on Goa Plants I, II, & III for the US) and **₹476 crore on R&D**. Yet, instead of clearing the FDA hurdle, Goa Plant II & III got hit with **OAI status and a fresh USFDA Warning Letter in June/December 2024**, while management simultaneously launched a 100%-owned OTC consumer subsidiary (**Warren Remedies**) to market *Sensodent* directly to consumers—spending heavily on TV ads, people, and supply chain with weak initial sales.

In his **January 4, 2025 *Economic Times* interview**, Aditya Khemka publicly announced his exit:
> **Aditya Khemka (Verbatim):** *"Recent exits – Indoco Remedies, as our thesis on recovery in the US business has not played out as expected. In their recent US FDA inspection, Unit II & III retained its OAI status; this will lead to a longer gestation period for the US business to bounce back."*

#### **3. Sajal Kapoor’s Exact Post-Mortem on Indoco Remedies (*"F25: Post Results Analysis"*, May 23, 2025)**
Remarkably, **Sajal Kapoor** also tracked Indoco Remedies (both Sajal Kapoor and Aditya Khemka asked back-to-back questions on Indoco’s Q2 FY21 concall!) and published a blunt 3-point post-mortem on Indoco in his **May 23, 2025 Substack essay**:
> **Sajal Kapoor (Verbatim from *Antifragile Thinking*, May 23, 2025):**
> *"According to the Q4 F25 update, Indoco faces 3 major issues:*
> *1. **Warren Remedies OTC Burn:** The 100% over-the-counter subsidiary of Warren Remedies saw little success in sales during its first year. Fixed costs (people, infrastructure, supply chain, and advertising investments) and low sales have created significant negative operating leverage in this division.*
> *2. **Manufacturing Infrastructure Remediation:** Oral plant master manufacturing plan execution negatively affected product delivery to Europe and the US (shutdowns).*
> *3. **Sterile Plant Warning Letter:** After an OAI classification, the sterile Goa plant received a warning notice in June 2024. This incident prevented the supply and exports of various products to the US.*
> ***Pharma businesses are like any cyclical business with high fixed costs, and underutilisation leads to a 'penalty,' as reflected in losses... The cost of remediation and cash loss consistently exceeds the initial, upfront cost of compliance.***"*

*(Note: While Indoco subsequently received EU-GMP clearance for Goa Plant II in July 2025 and saw US sales rebound 62.2% off a low base in Q1 FY27, its domestic formulations stayed flat at ₹204 Cr in Q1 FY27 and its consolidated ROE collapsed to near zero/-0.5% during the FY25–FY26 trough—completely validating both Khemka’s and Sajal’s decision to step aside!)*
