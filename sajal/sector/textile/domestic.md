Domestic Non-Branded / B2B Players (>70% Domestic Revenue)

• SBC Exports – Domestic garment manufacturing/trading alongside manpower and IT services (~95%+ domestic).
• Vishal Fabrics – B2B denim and dyed fabric manufacturer supplying primarily to domestic garmenters and brands (~85–90% domestic).

• Fascinate Textiles – Order-driven B2B readymade garment manufacturer supplying domestic wholesalers and retailers (~95%+ domestic).
