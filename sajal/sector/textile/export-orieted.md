
Balanced Mix: Export & Domestic (Around 40–60% / 50–50%)
--------------------------------------------------------

These companies maintain a substantial revenue split between international exports and Indian domestic operations.
• Arvind Ltd – Roughly 45–50% exports (woven/denim fabrics and garments to global brands) and 50–55% domestic (fabrics, advanced materials, and domestic apparel).
• Zodiac Clothing Co – Roughly 50–55% exports (menswear garment exports to Europe/US) and 45–50% domestic (branded retail via Zodiac, ZOD!, and Z3).
• Active Clothing Co – Designs and manufactures flat-knit sweaters and jackets for global brands alongside a large domestic brand-distribution and retail business (~40–50% export / 50–60% domestic).
• Filatex Fashions – Manufactures cotton/woolen socks for both international private-label buyers and domestic retail/wholesale (~40–50% export / 50–60% domestic)


Export-Oriented Players (>70% Export Revenue)

These companies are pure-play or predominantly B2B contract manufacturers for global fashion, kidswear, and hosiery brands.
• Pearl Global Industries – ~90%+ export revenue supplying global apparel retailers across the US, UK, and Europe.
• Gokaldas Exports – ~85%+ export revenue as one of India's largest readymade garment exporters to North America and Europe.
• Kitex Garments – ~90%+ export revenue, specializing in infant and kids' apparel for major US and European retailers.
• S P Apparels – ~80–85% export revenue, focused on knitted infant and children's wear for UK and European retail chains.
• Brahm Virat Industries (formerly Virat Industries) – Operates as a 100% Export Oriented Unit (EOU) manufacturing knitted socks primarily for European markets (~85–90%+ exports).
• Meenakshi (India) – ~89% export revenue, manufacturing bottom wear and outerwear for global apparel brands
