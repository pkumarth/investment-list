=> Core Financial Screen Rules for Your Analysis

When inputting these commodity names into your financial modeling sheet on October 31, 2026, use these three screening filters to identify structural margin safety:
1. Asset Turnover on Gross Block: Look for companies achieving a Fixed Asset Turnover above 1.5x–2.0x. Because gross margins are thin, the business must sweat its physical assets rapidly to deliver an acceptable Return on Capital Employed (ROCE).
2. Inventory Days & Receivable Drag: Commodity plays are forced to offer longer credit terms to bulk corporate buyers. A widening working capital cycle is the first warning sign of inventory dumping or severe price erosion in the channel.
3. China Sourcing Dependency: Assess what percentage of their raw intermediates or KSMs are imported. If a company is not backward integrated, an upward price spike in raw Chinese chemical baselines will completely compress its operating EBITDA margins.
