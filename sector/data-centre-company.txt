Interesting observation from the Q2FY27 conference call.

Aeroflex is evolving from a flexible hose manufacturer into a broader supplier of liquid-cooling solutions for AI data centres.

Here's what's changing:

1. Liquid cooling is becoming a major business. It contributed 24% of H1FY27 revenue. AI data centres need efficient cooling, creating a growing opportunity for Aeroflex's skid assemblies and engineered hose solutions.

2. Capacity expansion is accelerating. The company plans to invest ₹202 crore to increase hose capacity from 20 million to 30 million metres annually and skid capacity from 15,000 to 30,000 units by March 2028.

Management expects these additional capacities to generate ₹500–550 crore of annual revenue potential at peak utilization.

3. International expansion could unlock better realizations. Discussions for liquid-cooling supplies to international markets have reached an advanced stage with an existing customer. Management expects international realizations to be higher than domestic levels.

4. Moving beyond its existing products. Aeroflex is developing complementary products for liquid-cooling infrastructure, potentially allowing it to supply more components per data-centre project.

5. Hyd-Air merger could improve efficiency. Integrating Hyd-Air's manufacturing capabilities could reduce operational duplication and help Aeroflex sell more fittings through its existing customer relationships.

6. Automation is strengthening manufacturing capabilities. Welding automation aims to improve productivity, reduce manual dependency and lower product rejection.

The bigger picture: Aeroflex is increasing its exposure to AI infrastructure while expanding manufacturing capacity and broadening its product portfolio.
