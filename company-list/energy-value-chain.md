# Indian Electrical Equipment & Clean Energy Capital Goods: Sector, Segment & Moat Classification (66 Companies)

## Executive Overview: The Commodity-to-Moat Spectrum

The **66 Electrical Equipment & Clean Energy Capital Goods companies** span **8 distinct operational sub-sectors**. Across this universe, business models divide cleanly along three structural tiers:

1. **Moated / IP-Protected Businesses (18%–38% OPM):** High-voltage grid technology (HVDC, GIS), utility-approved transformer bushings, custom precision aero/nuclear components, industrial automation/aftermarket spares, and backward-integrated solar cell/module manufacturers protected by ALMM/DCR trade barriers.
2. **Semi-Moated / Qualification-Led Businesses (12%–22% OPM):** EHV/specialty transformers, smart metering AMISP contracts, industrial gearboxes, and power electronics where CPRI/utility type-testing and OEM design-ins create high switching costs.
3. **Commodity / Conversion & Tender-EPC Businesses (4%–12% OPM):** Winding wires, bare conductors, galvanized steel structures, and turnkey T&D EPC where margins depend on raw-material pass-through, working capital turns, and L1 tender pricing.
